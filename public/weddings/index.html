About usEst. Metro Vancouver

Vivid Stories is a photo and film team based in Metro Vancouver, and we shoot weddings the way they actually happen. There's very little posing and a lot of watching. With 2 to 4 of us on the day, someone is always in the right spot when a real moment shows up.

Good to know06 questions
Are you free on our date?

Send us your date and venue and we will confirm.

How do we book?

A 40% non-refundable retainer holds your date. You can split the rest into instalments, all paid 30 days before the wedding.

Can we build our own package?

Yes. Tell us the hours, crew and films you want and we will price it.

Do you travel?

Yes. Metro Vancouver has no travel fee. Anywhere else is quoted separately.

When do we get everything?

Photos in 6 weeks, films in 10 to 12, and the documentary in 14 to 16. Express delivery ($500) gets you photos in 3 weeks and films in 6.

What's in the gallery?

A private online gallery with full-resolution downloads, a print release and a year of hosting.
